### Upgrade impact
- Existing configurations are likely unaffected unless you adopt the new optional attributes or rely on export for journey outcome resources.
- Upgrading to v1.85.0 migrates `genesyscloud_recording_media_retention_policy` state for `team_ids` from sets to lists automatically; review plans if you manage team-based retention conditions.
- `genesyscloud_outbound_dnclist` resources that omit `entries` should no longer risk wiping phone numbers on unrelated updates; omit `entries` only when you intentionally do not want Terraform to manage list contents.
- Preview outbound campaigns can opt into `preview_auto_end`; quality evaluation and survey forms expose new versioning and scoring fields that appear in state after refresh.
- Re-export org configurations if you depend on journey outcome export or greeting audio in a specific WAV format.
- WebRTC phone creates for users who already have an assigned phone should succeed by adopting the existing device instead of erroring.
